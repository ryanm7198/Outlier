Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Outlier (outliertrivia.app) is a trivia game. This page explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it plain and short.

Who is responsible

Outlier is run by Outlier Games, based in the United Kingdom. For any privacy question or request, email RyanM7198@yahoo.co.uk. In data protection terms, Outlier Games is the controller of the information described here.

We do not sell your information, we do not show adverts, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Legal basis

  • Contract: your email, play history and subscription details, so we can provide the account and subscription you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests: anonymous answer counts, leaderboards, keeping the service secure and preventing abuse (for example rate limits on sign-in emails). We believe this is proportionate and has little impact on you.

What is stored on your device

Outlier does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores a few things in your browser's local storage so the game works: your sign-in session, your chosen name, your player ID, your daily progress and streak, and your recent categories. This is needed for the game to function. You can clear it any time in your browser settings, which will sign you out.

Who else handles your information

  • Render hosts the website and its database.
  • Resend delivers the sign-in emails (it sees your email address and the message).
  • Stripe processes subscription payments. Your card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store them.
  • Google Fonts supplies the game's typeface, so your browser contacts Google to fetch it (which includes your IP address).

These providers may process information outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK's approved transfer mechanisms.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes and sessions after 90 days.
  • Leaderboard entries and anonymous answer counts are kept as long as the game runs.
  • Your email and play history are kept while your account exists. Ask us and we will delete them.
  • Payment records are kept as long as the law requires for accounting.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive a copy. Email RyanM7198@yahoo.co.uk from the address on your account and we will respond within a month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Outlier is not aimed at children under 13, and accounts and subscriptions are for people aged 18 or over, or with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top. See also our terms of use.
